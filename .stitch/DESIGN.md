# Design System: Clinical Sanctuary (V2 - Unified Dark)

## 1. Atmosphere & Vibe
- **Core Aesthetic:** Premium, High-Contrast Dark Mode, "Clinical Sanctuary".
- **Visual Style:** Deep layering, frosted glass effects (glassmorphism), neon-tinted accents.
- **Emotion:** Ultra-professional, secure, cutting-edge, calm.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Background (Base):** `#07111c` (Deep Obsidian / Navy Black).
- **Surface (Primary):** `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)` with backdrop blur.
- **Surface (Utility):** `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.5)` for inputs and inner-cards.
- **Primary (Action):** Navy Gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #1E3A8A, #10243a)`.
- **Accent (Priority):** Gold/Amber (`#d8ad34` or `#e8c766`) — used for high-importance metrics (ZMF), primary CTA glows.
- **Success:** Emerald/Cyan-Green (`#10B981`) — used for ZSAF, healthy metrics.
- **Destructive:** Rose-Red (`#EF4444`) — alerts and SOS states.
- **Text (Primary):** White / Slate 50.
- **Text (Secondary/Muted):** Slate 400/500.

## 3. Typography
- **Primary:** Inter (or Public Sans) for body.
- **Secondary:** Manrope for headers (wide, premium tracking).
- **Styles:** Uppercase "Eyebrow" breadcrumbs (e.g., `SAÚDE · BIOMETRIA`) in tracking-widest font-bold.

## 4. Shapes & Structure
- **Roundness:** `rounded-[20px]` or `rounded-full` for buttons. 
- **Borders:** Subtle `border-border/50` (1px translucent edge).
- **Ambient Lighting:** Subtle radial gradients `bg-mesh` behind cards to create depth.

## 5. Design System Notes for Stitch Generation
**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- **Mode:** DARK MODE ONLY (Do not use white or cream backgrounds).
- **Theme:** Clinical Sanctuary (Premium Glassmorphism).
- **Palette:** Background - #07111c, Primary - #1E3A8A, Accent - #d8ad34.
- **Components:** High-fidelity cards with internal blur, pill-shaped buttons with subtle gradients, wide-spaced data tables with ultra-clean typography.
- **Consistency:** Every page must include the "Eyebrow" section header in uppercase.
